server.c        networking only: socket, bind, listen, accept two players,
   │            signal(SIGPIPE, SIG_IGN), srand once, then hands both
   │            sockets to the session
   ▼
session.c       the game master: setup questions, coin flip, relays messages
   │            between the two sockets, reads the clock once per loop,
   │            announces the winner
   │
   ├── word_bank.c     loads data/*.json, picks a random chain,
   │                   validates player-made lists (Classic)
   ├── game.c          the chain, hints, guess checking, scores,
   │                   view_game, coin flip
   └── mode.h          the contract the session talks through
        ├── blitz_mode.c     Blitz referee: 30 s clock per player
        └── classic_mode.c   Classic rules: wrong guess ends the turn
                             (to be moved onto mode.h)
