Blitz Gameplay

Setup
- The player picked Blitz mode.
- The game master (session.c) provides each player a list of 30 words in the format word1\nword2\n....
- Each player works through their own list.
- A coin flip decides which player guesses first (first_player).

Word List Visibility
- The full list is shown all at once
- The game master only sends the visible words to each client, so the hidden words cannot be read ahead of time.

Rules
- Each player has their own 30-second timer. It runs only on their turn and halts on the opponent's turn.
- Hint level has no cap.
- Each player's number of correct words is stored.
- An empty, misspelled, or incomplete guess counts as a wrong guess.

Coin Flip
- The game master randomly picks one of the two players to make the call.
- The picked player chooses heads or tails.
- The game master flips the coin.
- If the flip matches the player's call, that player is first_player. If it does not match, the other player is first_player.
- The flip is done once, by the game master, and the result is sent to both clients so they agree on who starts.
- The coin flip only decides who opens the game. Every turn after that follows the turn rules below.

Step-by-step
1. The game master prepares the word lists and reveals the player view of the chain.
2. The game master picks a random player to call heads or tails, flips the coin, and the result determines first_player. The game starts with that player's turn.
3. The active player guesses the current word on their list.
4. Correct guess: the player's score goes up by one and the visible window moves forward by one word. They keep the turn.
5. Wrong guess:
    - A hint (letter) is appended to their current word.
    - The turn switches to the other player on both clients.
    - The wrong player's timer halts, and the other player's timer resumes.
    - When the turn comes back, the player retries the same word (now with more of it revealed) and their timer resumes from where it stopped, not from 30.
6. If one player's timer hits zero first, that player is out. The other player keeps playing alone, with their timer still running, until it hits zero or they finish their list.

Ending
- A player finishes their list: the game stops immediately, results are tallied, and that player wins.
- Both timers reach zero with no one finished: the player with the most correct words out of 30 wins.
- Tie: either both finished at the same instant or nobody finished and the scores are equal. Both players win.
